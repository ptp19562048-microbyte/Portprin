# Portprin

Portprin is a friendly, browser-based desktop environment: comfortable, easy, and fast.

## What you can do

- Launch Notes, Files, Browser, Calculator, Tasks, Calendar, and Settings from the desktop or app dock.
- Save notes and tasks in your browser, calculate expressions, preview starter files, and open web searches or links in a new tab.
- Personalize the desktop with Aurora or Sunset wallpapers.

Your notes, tasks, wallpaper, and sound preference are stored locally in your browser. Portprin is a web desktop experience, not a replacement for your computer's operating system.
